## On this page
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

I consider Marksmanship the foundation of most Hunter builds. It improves our ranged damage, extends our reach, reduces the mana cost of key abilities, and offers a party-wide attack power buff.

Because Aimed Shot is central to almost every build I recommend, I usually commit at least 11 talent points to Marksmanship. At level 60, that leaves at most 40 of our 51 points for Beast Mastery and Survival combined. This limits how deeply we can invest in those two trees, even when one of them is the main focus of the build.

Marksmanship can therefore serve as the foundation for many different builds, but that near-mandatory investment also constrains our choices elsewhere.

The tradeoff is that I find Marksmanship less interesting to customize. Once I have chosen how far to invest, many of the remaining choices are straightforward. A few talents offer little value for the way I play, so there are fewer competing options than in the other trees.

Let’s look at the talents in detail.

## Talents

### Improved Concussive Shot
{: #improved-concussive-shot }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Improved Concussive Shot on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19415)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, gives Concussive Shot a 20% chance to stun its target for 3 seconds.

**My assessment**

The stun is unpredictable, but it can make a decisive difference when it triggers. For PvP, solo play, and many dungeon builds, this is where I prefer to spend my first five Marksmanship points.

### Efficiency
{: #efficiency }

**My rating: B-tier** · [Efficiency on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19420)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, reduces the mana cost of your shots and stings by 10%.

**My assessment**

Helps sustain damage in longer fights. I usually choose it for raids or whenever I have little use for Improved Concussive Shot.

### Improved Hunter's Mark
{: #improved-hunters-mark }

**My rating: D-tier** · [Improved Hunter's Mark on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19425)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, increases the ranged attack power bonus from Hunter’s Mark by 15%.

**My assessment**

Rank 4 Hunter’s Mark grants 110 ranged attack power. A 15% increase amounts to 16.5 additional ranged attack power before any in-game rounding, or only 3.3 per talent point. Other Hunters benefit too, but I still consider the gain too small for five points.

### Lethal Shots
{: #lethal-shots }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Lethal Shots on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19431)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, grants an additional 5 percentage points of critical strike chance with ranged weapons.

**My assessment**

A useful increase to our critical strike chance and damage. The bonus applies only to ranged attacks.

### Aimed Shot
{: #aimed-shot }

**My rating: S-tier** · [Aimed Shot on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19434)

**What it changes**

Unlocks Aimed Shot.

**My assessment**

A core part of my damage rotation and a major source of burst damage. I consider it essential for raiding and valuable in most other activities. It shares a cooldown with Arcane Shot.

### Improved Arcane Shot
{: #improved-arcane-shot }

**My rating: D-tier** · [Improved Arcane Shot on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19458)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, reduces Arcane Shot’s cooldown by 1 second, from 6 to 5 seconds.

**My assessment**

Arcane Shot remains useful for pulling and dealing some damage while moving. However, since I use Aimed Shot for my main damage rotation, reducing Arcane Shot’s cooldown offers little value to my usual builds.

### Hawk Eye
{: #hawk-eye }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Hawk Eye on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19500)

**What it changes**

At 3/3, extends the range of your ranged attacks by 6 yards, from 35 to 41 yards.

**My assessment**

Extra range gives us more room to engage and kite enemies. I particularly value it in PvP, but the positioning advantage is useful in PvE too.

### Improved Serpent Sting
{: #improved-serpent-sting }

**My rating: C-tier** · [Improved Serpent Sting on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19468)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, increases Serpent Sting’s damage by 10%.

**My assessment**

Because Serpent Sting’s base damage does not scale with our ranged attack power, I find the additional damage difficult to justify. The ability still has uses outside raids.

Where a raid operates under a debuff limit, follow the group’s debuff assignments rather than applying Serpent Sting automatically.

### Mortal Shots
{: #mortal-shots }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Mortal Shots on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19490)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, increases the critical strike damage bonus of your ranged attacks by 30%.

**My assessment**

A normal ranged critical strike deals twice the damage of a normal hit. At 5/5, Mortal Shots increases the bonus portion from 100% to 130%, so the critical strike deals 230% of a normal hit’s damage.

The higher your effective critical strike chance, the greater the average benefit. At 20% crit, this adds about 5% to average damage from affected ranged attacks; at 40%, it adds about 8.6%. These examples assume ordinary critical strikes without other critical-damage modifiers. They do not apply to melee attacks or pet damage, so the increase to total combined damage will be smaller.

### Scatter Shot
{: #scatter-shot }

**My rating: S-tier** · [Scatter Shot on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19503)

**What it changes**

Unlocks a short-range shot that deals 50% weapon damage and disorients its target for 4 seconds. Damage breaks the disorient, and using the ability stops your attack.

**My assessment**

If we don’t have it, we miss it. A flexible shot with both defensive and offensive uses. I mainly use it as an interrupt or to set up my next action, rather than as sustained crowd control.

- Set up a trap in PvP.• Stop a loose enemy until the tank can pick it up in a dungeon.• Respond to a Mage who Blinks into close range.• Interrupt an enemy’s cast or create a brief opening to reposition.

### Barrage
{: #barrage }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Barrage on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=24691)

**What it changes**

At 3/3, increases the damage of Multi-Shot and Volley by 15%.

**My assessment**

Multi-Shot is part of our core rotation, so increasing its damage helps against both single targets and groups. I mainly take this talent for Multi-Shot; I place less value on the Volley bonus.

### Improved Scorpid Sting
{: #improved-scorpid-sting }

**My rating: D-tier** · [Improved Scorpid Sting on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19494)

**What it changes**

At 3/3, Scorpid Sting also reduces the target’s Stamina by 30% of the amount of Strength it removes.

**My assessment**

Another talent I find difficult to justify. With maximum-rank Scorpid Sting, the stamina reduction is about 20 points, or roughly 200 health, for the effect’s duration. Even with this talent, I would usually prefer Viper Sting against mana users or Serpent Sting against melee opponents.

### Ranged Weapon Specialization
{: #ranged-weapon-specialization }

**My rating: A-tier** · [Ranged Weapon Specialization on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19511)

**What it changes**

At 5/5, increases your ranged weapon damage by 5%.

**My assessment**

A direct increase to our ranged damage and, alongside Mortal Shots, one of the main reasons I invest deeply in Marksmanship.

### Trueshot Aura
{: #trueshot-aura }

**My rating: S-tier** · [Trueshot Aura on Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19506)

**What it changes**

At spell rank 3, grants 100 attack power to party members within 45 yards.

**My assessment**

A useful contribution to the damage of our entire party, including ourselves and our pet. In a raid, the aura benefits our party rather than every member of the raid.

It is one of the few party-wide buffs we bring, alongside a wolf’s Furious Howl, Aspect of the Wild, and Aspect of the Pack. I usually bring this talent when my raid party includes several physical damage dealers.
