# Marksmanship
{: .no_toc }

<figure class="hunter-art hunter-portrait">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/talents/marksmanship.png' | relative_url }}" alt="A Hunter aiming a rifle from a mountain overlook" width="496" height="568">
</figure>

I consider Marksmanship our core specialization. A few remarks on MM:

- Everything we want to do becomes better with Marksmanship. It increases our personal ranged damage output significantly, it increases our range, it makes our main abilities more mana-efficient and it allows us to provide a party-wide attack power bonus.

- In almost all cases, we want to spend at least 11 points in MM; otherwise we don’t have access to Aimed Shot and our damage output suffers significantly. This leaves at most 40 points for Beast Mastery and Survival combined, limiting how much we can invest in the other two specializations.

- At the same time, MM is our most boring specialization. There are basically no choices to be made. We don’t want to spend more than 31 points in it and there is not much flexibility, other than a few talent options. In contrast to the other two specializations, MM has some truly bad talents which we never want to pick up.

For these reasons, MM is a staple of all our builds, except some niche experimental builds. At the same time, it’s not very exciting and there are not many meaningful choices to be made. Let’s examine the talents.
