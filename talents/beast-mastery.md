# Beast Mastery
{: .no_toc }

<figure class="hunter-art hunter-portrait">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/talents/beast-mastery.png' | relative_url }}" alt="An Orc Hunter with a wolf companion in a forest" width="524" height="568">
</figure>

As the name suggests, Beast Mastery focuses primarily on your animal companion. Before looking at the talents, it helps to understand a pet’s baseline stats. The table below uses a “neutral” pet without family-specific modifiers. Your chosen family changes these values, so treat them as a baseline for comparison. The baseline assumes an untrained pet with no pet-training bonuses, family modifiers, or buffs, and happy (green) happiness.

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 3,052 HP |
| Armor | 4,014 |
| Physical damage reduction from armor against a level 60 enemy | 42.2% |
| Base auto-attack DPS | 51.25 DPS |
| Base critical strike chance | 5% |
| Focus regeneration per second | 5 focus/second |

A few additional remarks: A faster attack speed does not, by itself, mean higher auto-attack DPS. For example, 51 damage every second and 102 damage every 2 seconds both average 51 DPS. Your pet possesses base stats, just as players do, including Stamina, Strength, and Agility. As I understand it, these attributes work as they do for a Warrior: an additional point of Strength gives 2 attack power, 20 Agility gives 1% critical strike chance, and 10 Stamina gives 100 HP.

Beast Mastery improves your pet’s damage, durability, movement speed, and focus regeneration. It also adds tools such as a stun and a powerful damage cooldown, while several talents improve the Hunter’s own abilities.

In raids and PvP, I mainly value the tree for its pet damage and the utility of its key abilities.

## Reasons to invest in Beast Mastery

- Increase the damage you and your pet deal together.

- Help your pet tank enemies in the open world and dungeons, and survive dangerous raid encounters.

- Gain specific utility, such as improved aspects or Intimidation’s pet-delivered stun.

Let’s evaluate all the Beast Mastery talents.
