# Beast Mastery
{: .no_toc }

<figure class="hunter-art hunter-portrait">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/talents/beast-mastery.png' | relative_url }}" alt="An Orc Hunter with a wolf companion in a forest" width="524" height="568">
</figure>

As its name suggests, this talent tree focuses mostly on improving the animal companion, your pet. Let’s first have a look at a pet’s base stats. Every pet has certain modifiers depending on the family it belongs to, but let’s start with a “neutral” pet, independent of pet family. Whatever family you choose, it will change the numbers – but not drastically in the big picture. The baseline assumes an untrained pet with no pet-training bonuses, family modifiers, or buffs, and happy (green) happiness.

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Health | 3,052 HP |
| Armor | 4,014 |
| Physical damage reduction from armor against a level 60 enemy | 42.2% |
| Base auto-attack DPS | 51.25 DPS |
| Base critical strike chance | 5% |
| Focus regeneration | 5 focus per second |

A few additional remarks: A faster attack speed does not, by itself, mean higher auto-attack DPS. For example, 51 damage every second and 102 damage every 2 seconds both average 51 DPS. Your pet possesses base stats, just as players do, including Stamina, Strength, and Agility. As I understand it, these attributes work as they do for a Warrior: an additional point of Strength gives 2 attack power, 20 Agility gives 1% critical strike chance, and 10 Stamina gives 100 HP.

Talents in Beast Mastery increase your pet’s offense, defense, movement speed, and focus regeneration, and give it additional utility in the form of a stun, a powerful DPS cooldown and immunity to crowd control (CC). There are also a few talents which affect the Hunter directly. All in all, Beast Mastery talents can greatly increase your pet’s damage and therefore meaningfully add to your own damage output, even when the Hunter has acquired the highest level of gear. While solo adventuring or in dungeons, it can add to your defense, because it can soak up damage with its ability to aggro and tank mobs. But in a raid or PvP setting, it does not meaningfully add to your defensive capabilities and serves mainly as a damage increase.

## Put talent points into Beast Mastery if

- You want to increase the combined damage output of you and your animal companion

- You want to increase your pet’s survivability so that it can tank enemies in the open world and dungeons and better survive in raids

- You want specific utility like improved aspects (Aspect of the Cheetah, Aspect of the Monkey, Aspect of the Hawk) or a 3-second stun that is delivered by your pet (Intimidation)

Let’s evaluate all the Beast Mastery talents.
