# Survival
{: .no_toc }

## On this page
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

The talent tree focused on improving utility and survivability, but also increases our damage output in various ways. Survival does what the name suggests – it bolsters our defense and durability, especially against melee opponents and complements MM and BM by rounding out our weakness in melee. It offers passive bonuses as well as active abilities to aid our survival. Throughout the tree we also find talents to increase our damage output and when we invest enough points into it, we can gain access to talents powerful enough to rival MMs damage increases. However, we don’t gain a group-wide damage increase like Trueshot Aura and therefore deep Survival builds are more suited for solo adventuring, farming, dungeons or pvp instead of high-end raiding, since we should never put our own damage output above a group-wide increase that benefits the whole squad.

I really like Survival because it has lots of strong and competing talents (and no useless ones) which gives us the opportunity to make the appropriate choices for the specific situation we want to tackle.

## Put points into Survival

- For situational damage increases against certain types of enemies, when melee weaving, when we include traps into our rotation. Additionally, Lightning Reflexes enables a well-rounded and versatile build that aims at maximizing attack power and crit chance by stacking agility, while picking up all the defensive and Cc benefits included in Survival.

- To increase survivability, especially against melee aggressors, by gaining active abilities and passive bonuses to mitigate and soak damage or straight up avoiding it by slowing and rooting enemies.

- To gain access to Entrapment which transforms Frost Trap into a powerful zoning tool to hinder enemies in choke points/areas which is generally strong, but especially in PvP.

## Talents

### [Monster Slaying](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=24295)

**What it changes:** At 3/3 provides 3% extra damage as well as 3% extra crit damage against Beasts, Giants and Dragonkin.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Point by Point a strong talent to against applicable foes. Good while leveling, dungeons, farming specific mobs and even early raids.

### [Humanoid Slaying](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19153)

**What it changes:** At 3/3 provides 3% extra damage as well as 3% extra crit damage against Humanoids.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Like Monster Slaying, strong talent where it applies. In this case also in PvP.

### [Deflection](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19300)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 provides 5% additional chance to Parry.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. This increases our base chance to Parry to 10%. Good defensive talent, specifically against melee enemies in PvP. Synergizes well with Counter Attack, a later talent.

### [Entrapment](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19390)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives Immolation Trap, Frost Trap, and Explosive Trap a 25% chance to entrap enemies, preventing them from moving for 5 sec.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. Frankly, this talent is overpowered (and will be nerved in WoW Forever). Specifically, its interaction with Frost Trap. With Explosive and Immolation Trap, the initial Trigger has a chance to root the target. However, Frost Trap has the chance to trigger the effect with every pulse/every 2 sec. This allows a Hunter to exercise heavy control on an area with slow and root effects. This is especially strong against Warriors and Rogues. The root is a magic effect and can therefore be dispelled by Priests and Paladins, but as long as enemies are inside Frost Traps area on effect, they can be affected again. Free Action Potion counters this effect, which reduces the talents effectiveness in coordinated PvP. But outside of that scenario its very strong.

### [Savage Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19160)

**What it changes:** At 2/2 increases our chance for a critical hit with Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite by 20%.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. This increases our chance to reliably hit very hard with our melee attacks when we need to. For example when we “melee-weave” in PvE scenarios or when an enemy engages toe-to-toe. A big Raptor Strike hit with a slow two-handed weapons may make our enemies regret the decision to come close.

### [Improved Wing Clip](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19235)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives our Wing Clip a 20% chance to root our target in place for 5 sec.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. In many scenarios I chose Entrapment over Wing Clip and it’s the stronger talent in battle ground situations. Since Wing Clip can be spammed frequently, this talent transforms it into a surprisingly reliable tool to snare a single enemy. Its probably the stronger choice in duels and gives you the possibility to snare melee targets without using Feign Death.

### [Clever Traps](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19245)

**What it changes:** At 2/2 increases the duration of Freezing and Frost traps effect by 30% and the damage of Immolation and Explosive traps by 30%.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. This further increases the interaction between Frost Trap and Entrapment. It also notably increases Freezing Trap’s duration. These are the more notable effects. In addition to that, our fire traps damage is increased. This is not to be underestimated: Immolation is our strongest single target dot and Explosive Trap our strongest ability against groups of mobs.

### [Survivalist](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19259)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives 10% additional health points.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. Gives health and let’s you survive longer. Mainly finds application in PvP since HP is very valuable there, specifically in duels.

### [Deterrence](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19263)

**What it changes:** Active talent. Increases chance to dodge and parry by 25% for 10 sec. 5 min CD.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Our strongest defensive CD. The CD is long, but balanced out how powerful this ability is. During its duration melee attackers will have a hard time dealing damage to you, while you get parry-hasted (your next swing occurs earlier when you parry an attack). This has strong synergy with Counter Attack.

### [Trap Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19377)

**What it changes:** At 2/2 decreases enemies’ chance to resist your traps by 10%.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. Traps hit chance is calculated like spell resistance. An enemy of the same level has a 4% chance to resist your traps. 1 point into Trap Mastery reduces this to 1%. It can never below 1%. I often put a point into it while in PvP to make my traps hit as reliably as possible. With 0 points, enemies of a higher level have a chance of 5% (lvl 61), 6% (lvl 62) and 17% (lvl 63, skull lvl) to resist. So put as many points into it as needed for your use case.

### [Surefooted](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=24283)

**What it changes:** At 3/3 gives 3% hit and 15% chance to resist slow and mobilizing effects.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. The talent serves a dual purpose. Hunters can build into it to compensate for a lack of hit on gear (as a Hunter you should aim for 9% in raids) which is especially useful for in earlier raid tiers. The second effect becomes useful in PvP, since the additional chance to resist slow and immobilize effects from abilities like Frost Nova, Frost Bolt, Hamstring and so on. 15% doesn’t sound like much but resisting those effects can mean victory or death and it will add up over time.

### [Improved Feign Death](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19287)

**What it changes:** At 2/2 decreases enemies’ chance to resist your Feign Death by 4%.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. The mechanics are the same like Trap Mastery. 2/2 reduces your enemies’ chance to resist Feign Death to 1%. Feign Death is a core ability in all game modes. We depend on it to get out of fights and survive situations we wouldn’t survive (and other classes will not), to lay down traps mid-fight as well as swapping our trinkets. This talent makes all of that more reliable and it is very noticeable over the long run.

### [Killer Instinct](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19373)

**What it changes:** At 3/3 gives 3% additional crit chance.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. Significant damage increase and if we go this deep into the Survival tree there is no reason to not pick it up. The talent is a requirement for our capstone talent Wyvern Sting.

### [Counterattack](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19306)

**What it changes:** A strike that becomes active after parrying an opponent's attack. This attack deals 40 damage and immobilizes the target for 5 sec. Counterattack cannot be blocked, dodged, or parried.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Synergy with Deflection and Deterrence. Without them it’s not a reliable defensive tool. However, while Deterrence is active, it’s a reliable tool to snare melee opponents to gain distance or set them up for a trap.

### [Lightning Reflexes](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=24297) (LR)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives an additional 15% agility.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. One reason to go deep into Survival is this talent and its interaction with our main stat, agility. A raid geared Hunter can achieve approximately 500-580 agility, before world buffs and consumes. This translates into 75-90 agility. Fully world buffed and consumed, this clocks in at around 130 agility in my personal case. In a raid scenario this means 260 ranged attack power and around 6% additional crit chance, only looking at the offensive side. Agility also increases dodge chance and armor value and therefore makes us more durable as well. This looks great on paper and it is. However, in a raid setting, a deep MM build with True Shot Aura is – in my opinion – still the way to go. While LR is all in all a slight boost to our personal damage, it does not beat TSA’s contribution to a raids overall damage, assuming you can apply it to at least 3 other players.

Still, LR is fun and I run it successfully in many builds in PvP and PvE, outside of raids.

### [Wyvern Sting](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19386)

**What it changes:** A stinging shot that puts the target to sleep for 12 sec. Any damage will cancel the effect. When the target wakes up, the Sting causes 300 Nature damage over 6 sec. Only usable out of combat. Only one Sting per Hunter can be active on the target at a time.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. Our Survival capstone talent. Its greatest drawback is, that when we take it, we can’t reach Intimidation and, more importantly, Scatter Shot. Scatter Shot has more flexibility and more use cases which makes it overall the more valuable ability. However, Wyvern Sting serves a different purpose. It is a 12 sec. long Cc that can be potentially fired from 41 yards away. Its value depends on how well it is used, but it can be potentially very impactful. Specifically in a supporting build.
