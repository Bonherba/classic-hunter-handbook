# Survival
{: .no_toc }

<figure class="hunter-art hunter-portrait">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/talents/survival.png' | relative_url }}" alt="A Hunter crouching in a forest with a blade" width="518" height="568">
</figure>

Survival improves our utility and durability, especially against melee opponents. It combines passive defenses with active tools that help us avoid damage, create distance, and control a fight. It also offers several ways to increase damage, with deeper investment unlocking powerful options such as Lightning Reflexes.

That combination makes Survival appealing for solo play, farming, dungeons, and PvP. In raids, I weigh its personal benefits against the party-wide contribution of Trueshot Aura. When several party members can benefit from the aura, I generally prefer to invest deeply in Marksmanship.

What I enjoy most about Survival is the number of worthwhile choices. Several talents compete for the same points, giving me room to adapt the build to the situation.

## Reasons to invest in Survival

- Increase damage against particular enemy types, strengthen melee attacks, or make traps a larger part of your playstyle. A deeper investment can unlock Lightning Reflexes for an Agility-focused build.

- Improve your ability to survive melee pressure through passive defenses, defensive cooldowns, slows, and roots.

- Unlock Entrapment to make Frost Trap a powerful tool for controlling contested areas and choke points, especially in PvP.
