# Introduction and Tier List
{: .no_toc }

## How to evaluate a talent

Talent trees decisively define how classes are played. While leveling, from level 10 onward, we are awarded one talent point per level and can use it immediately to improve a certain aspect of our class or gain new abilities. At level 60, we will have 51 talent points to freely spend on our specialization. Some talents have point requirements or prerequisite talents. For example, Aimed Shot only becomes available when at least 10 points have been invested in Marksmanship. Usually, there is a powerful and gameplay-defining talent to choose once a player invests their 11th, 21st or 31st point into one specialization.

The Hunter is a specialized ranged damage dealer and our talents don’t change the class’s core gameplay. Rather, they strengthen certain aspects of our class (such as the animal companion), build on our strengths, or compensate for our weakness in melee combat. Exploring and experimenting with the different talents is a lot of fun and helps us understand the facets of our class. Many guides refer to a handful of builds and present them as the best or only viable option. But it’s important to understand that no single build is best at everything! The optimal talents always depend on the situation at hand and every build you encounter should be understood as a general starting point to build on and adapt to your own playstyle and circumstances.

## How I rate talents

Just for the fun of it, I try to assess and rate all the talents on a tier list.

| Rating | How I use it |
| :--- | :--- |
| **S-tier** | Build-defining talents. I build around them or consider them essential to the playstyle I am pursuing. |
| **A-tier** | Strong talents that I usually take when I invest far enough in the tree to reach them. |
| **B-tier** | Valuable options that compete with other worthwhile choices. Whether I take them depends on the build. |
| **C-tier** | Niche talents, or talents whose benefits are usually too limited to justify the points. |
| **D-tier** | Talents I would not take; I consider the alternatives a better use of the points. |
