# Marksmanship
{: .no_toc }

## On this page
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

I consider Marksmanship our core specialization. A few remarks on MM:

Everything we want to do becomes better with Marksmanship. It increases our personal ranged damage output significantly, it increases our range, it makes our main abilities more mana efficient and it allows us to provide a group-wide attack power bonus.

In almost all cases we want to spend at least 11 points into MM, otherwise we don’t have access to Aimed Shot and our damage output suffers significantly. This gives MM flexibility the other specializations don’t have.

At the same time, MM is our most boring specialization. There are basically no choices to be made. We don’t want to spend more than 31 points into it and there is not much flexibility, other than a few talent options. In contrast to the other two specializations, MM has some truly bad talents which we never want to pick up.

For these reasons, MM is a staple of all our builds, except some niche experimental builds. At the same time, it’s not very exciting and there are not many meaningful choices to be made. Let’s examine the talents.

## Talents

### [Improved Concussive Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19415)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives Concussive Shot a 20% chance to stun the target for 3 sec.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Not a reliable stun, but nevertheless very impactful when it triggers. In PvP scenarios and even in dungeons and solo adventuring the first five points in MM go into this talent.

### [Efficiency](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19420)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 reduces the mana cost of our shots and stings by 10%.

**My assessment:** B-Tier. Makes us more efficient and let us sustain our damage in longer fights. In raid settings and when we don’t have use for improved Concussive Shot, we put the first five points into Efficiency.

### [Improved Hunter's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19425)

**What it changes:** At 5/5. Increases Hunter’s Mark attack power bonus by 15%.

**My assessment:** D-Tier. Our highest rank of Hunter’s Mark (Rank 4) gives 110 ranged attack power. This translates into 16 additional attack power or less then 4 attack power per talent point. It gets a bit better since every Hunter in the raid benefits from it but its still minor.

### [Lethal Shots](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19431)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 gives additional 5% chance to crit with ranged weapons.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Significant damage increase. Mind that it only increases ranged attacks.

### [Aimed Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19434)

**What it changes:** Gives access to Aimed Shot.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. Core part of our damage rotation and without it we lose a significant percentage of damage output. Necessary for raiding and also crucial for other environments. A huge contributor to our burst damage. It shares CD with Arcane Shot.

### [Improved Arcane Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19458)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 reduces Arcane Shot’s coold down by 1 sec., down to 5 sec.

**My assessment:** D-Tier. Arcane Shot is mostly used for pulling or if we want to do at least a bit of damage while moving. Reducing the CD is rather insignificant. If we spent points into Marksmanship we will pick up Aimed Shot which makes this ability obsolete.

### [Hawk Eye](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19500)

**What it changes:** At 3/3 increases the range of our attacks by 6 yards, from 35 to 41 yards.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Utility talent which enables us to engage and kite enemies in PvP from afar. While strong in PvP it is also notable in PvE.

### [Improved Serpent Sting](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19468)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 increases the damage of Serpent Sting by 10%.

**My assessment:** C-Tier. Serpent Sting sadly doesn’t scale with our gear and this makes the damage increase rather insignificant. The ability is still useful outside of raids. Don’t use it in raids due to the debuff limit on bosses.

### [Mortal Shots](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19490)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 increases our critical strike damage by 30%.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Our critical hits do 100% more damage and 5/5 Mortal Shots increases this bonus to 130%. This talent becomes stronger with higher crit chance. With a crit chance of 20% this translates into a 5% overall personal damage increase. With 40% crit chance (e.g. in a raid setting) this translates into almost 9% additional damage. This only affects ranged attacks.

### [Scatter Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19503)

**What it changes:** Gives access to Scatter Shot. A short-range shot that deals 50% weapon damage and disorients the target for 4 sec. Any damage caused will remove the effect. Turns off your attack when used.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. If we don’t have it, we miss it. A flexible and powerful shot to interrupt and setup enemies. Can be reliably used defensively and offensively in all kinds of scenarios. In contrary to Wyvern Sting this is not a Cc shot in itself, rather serves to setup up enemies to reliably trap them (PvP), to stop an aggressor in its tracks until a tank can pick it up (dungeons), to punish a Mage blinking into close range and generally interrupt enemies from using abilities.

### [Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=24691)

**What it changes:** Increases the damage done by your Multi-Shot and Volley spells by 15%.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Significant damage increase to our Multi-Shot which translates into more damage in cleave situations as well as single targets. Multi-Shot is a core part of our damage rotation and therefore our overall damage in all situations benefits from it. Since Volley doesn’t scale with our gear, it’s a minor increase there.

### [Improved Scorpid Sting](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19494)

**What it changes:** Reduces the Stamina of targets affected by your Scorpid Sting by 30% of the amount of Strength reduced.

**My assessment:** D-Tier. Another talent which there is few incentives to pick it. At max level this reduces an enemies’ stamina by 20 (200 health points) during its duration. Even with the talent, I don’t see us using this over Viper’s Sting on mana users or Serpent’s Sting on melee opponents.

### [Ranged Weapon Specialization](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19511)

**What it changes:** At 5/5 increases our ranged damage by 5%.

**My assessment:** A-Tier. Increases our personal ranged damage by 5% and therefore is the most significant personal damage increase in the Marksmanship specialization,  alongside Mortal Shots.

### [Trueshot Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=19506)

**What it changes:** Increases attack power of party members within 45 yards by 100.

**My assessment:** S-Tier. In a raid setting we usually have 2-3 melee attackers in our raid group. This also affects our pet and ourself and is a significant contribution to overall raid damage. This is one of the very few partywide buffs that we bring, aside the wolf’s Furious Howl, Aspect of Nature and Pack. We are expected to bring this in a raid setting.
