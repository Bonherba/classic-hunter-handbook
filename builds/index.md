# Build Library

The following page shows a variety of builds for different types of content. The builds should serve as a starting point and be adapted to your own situation and playstyle. I could think of adjustments to every build listed here, depending on different situations.

Each visual shows the allocated talents and their ranks. Click it, or use the link below it, to open the Warcraft Tavern calculator. Point allocations are shown in **Beast Mastery / Marksmanship / Survival** order.
