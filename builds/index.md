# Build Library

The following page shows a variety of builds for different types of content. The builds should serve as a starting point and be adapted to your own situation and playstyle. I could think of adjustments to every build listed here, depending on different situations.

Each visual shows the allocated talents and their ranks. Use **Open calculator here** to try a build within its build page, or click its image or external link to open Warcraft Tavern separately. The embedded view includes Warcraft Tavern’s full page. Point allocations are shown in **Beast Mastery / Marksmanship / Survival** order.
