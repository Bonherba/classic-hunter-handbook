**Talent allocation:** 20 / 31 / 0

This is the recommended raiding build (once you reach the 9% hit cap with your gear). It maximizes personal and pet damage and provides Trueshot Aura for your raid party. The build relies on your animal companion being alive, which takes practice but is doable in almost all situations. Naxxramas is the only raid where I can’t keep my pet alive for all bosses. Note: Your pet receives all World Buffs that are “popped”, but can’t gain the ones from dialogues. This means your pet can get Dragonslayer (Ony/Nef), Rend (also on Alliance) and HoH (Heart of Hakkar from Zul’Gurub) which will increase its damage significantly. In addition to that, there are more buffs that can be applied to the pet, namely scrolls and Mauri buffs. I will link to guides which go into detail.

## MM/Surv — Raiding Build Without the Hit Cap

**Talent allocation:** 2 / 31 / 18

This is an alternative raiding build, which neglects pet damage and instead goes into Survival and picks up some of the earlier damage buffs, but most importantly Surefooted, which provides 3% hit. This build is strong when you are early in your gear progression, since it allows you to reach the hit cap more easily, you can get accustomed to pet survivability and are not punished too badly when it dies. In addition to that, the slayer talents from Survival apply to many bosses in the first two raid tiers (giants, dragonkin, beasts and humanoids). With a few modifications you can tailor the build to open world farming and dungeon running and this way you have one build which performs well in most scenarios.
