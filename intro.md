# Talents in-depth
{: .no_toc }

## How to evaluate a talent

Talent trees decisively define how classes are played. During the leveling part we are rewarded with a point each level and can use it immediately to improve a certain aspect of our class or to gain new abilities. Usually there is powerful and gameplay defining talent to choose once a player invests their 11th, 21th or 31th point into one specialization. The Hunter is solely focused on ranged damage our talents don’t change the classes core gameplay, rather they strengthen certain aspects of our class (like the animal companion) or complement it by building on our strengths or bolstering our weakness. Exploring and experimenting with the different talents is a lot of fun and helps understand the facets of our class. Many guides refer to a handful of builds and present them as the best or only viable option. But it’s important to understand that there is no best build at everything! The optimal talents always depend on the situation at hand and every build you encounter should be understood as a general starting point on which to build on and adjust to your own playstyle and circumstances.

## My rating scale

I try to rate all the talents on a scale of D-S. D represents a talent I would not take under any circumstances, while S is a talent I would build around or always choose when going into the respective talent tree.
